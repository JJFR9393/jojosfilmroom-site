You don't just watch movies. You live in them.

You argue about them in the car. You walk out of the theatre needing someone to say, "Did you see that?" JoJo's Film Room is that someone, and that room. Every two weeks we break down the film of the month, pair it with a classic, and hand you the Verdict.

Meet JoJo
"A friend who has seen everything, and never makes you feel bad for not having seen it."

JoJo's Verdict

No stars. No thumbs. Just the truth.

Every film gets a Verdict on how to watch it, and a score out of five gold Reels on how good it is.

Rent itWorth a look when it's cheap and easy.
Stream itGood. Watch it when it lands on your service.
See it in a theatreMade for the big screen. Buy the ticket.
See it twiceEssential. Go back for everything you missed.
Five Reels. True North.The highest honour in the Film Room.
