Questions

Good to know.

What is the Visual Deck?

A designed companion for each episode: original scene diagrams, shot lists, character maps, fun-fact cards and JoJo's Verdict. Free listeners see a 3-slide preview; Room Members get the full deck.

Is the podcast free?

Every episode has a free version on Spotify: the Intro, Opening Act and Rising Action. The full episode, with the Climax, the Mailbag and the Conclusion, is for Founding Members on Patreon, along with the full Visual Deck, early episodes, a bonus episode on off weeks and the community perks.

What does 'Founding Member' mean?

The first 100 Room Members lock in $5 CAD a month for life, and their names appear in the credits of every Deck.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Memberships are managed through Patreon, where you can change or cancel whenever you like.

Are the party kits family-friendly?

Every kit includes a family-friendly version. Adult drinking-game versions are for those of legal drinking age; please drink responsibly.
