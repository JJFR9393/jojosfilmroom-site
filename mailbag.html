The Mailbag

Ask JoJo anything.

Questions are read and answered in the Climax of every episode. Room Members' questions go first.

Write to the show mailbag@jojosfilmroom.com

Tell us your first name and city if you'd like a shout-out. Keep it to one question per email so it has the best chance of making the show.

Press, guests and partnerships

Filmmakers, critics, fellow podcasters and partners: we'd love to hear from you. Email mailbag@jojosfilmroom.com and start your subject line with the right tag so your message reaches the right pile:

PRESS: interviews, features and media requests
GUEST: appearing on the show
PARTNERSHIP: sponsorships and collaborations

Example subject: "GUEST: Toronto filmmaker, new documentary"
